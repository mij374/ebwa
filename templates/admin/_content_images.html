Video

A YouTube or Vimeo video, shown where the lead photo would sit. The page shows a still picture with a play button and contacts YouTube or Vimeo only if a visitor presses play — so nobody is tracked for simply reading the page.

Paste the ordinary address from the browser's address bar — YouTube or Vimeo, either the long or the short form. Leave it empty to remove the video. Do not paste embed code: it would appear on the page as the code itself, and this box does the same job properly.

Photos

The first photo (lowest sort number) is the lead image. Every photo needs alt text — a short description for people using a screen reader.

{#- One photo may end up in any of the three presets, and they crop to different shapes, so the advice has to suit all of them: 1600px wide covers the largest box on a 2x screen, and keeping the subject central survives every crop. Measured slot widths are in the CLAUDE.md notes. -#} Recommended: about 1600px wide, landscape, subject near the middle. Straight off a phone is fine — photos are turned the right way up, stripped of location data and resized automatically.
Photos are cropped to fit, never squashed, and the shape depends on where they land: roughly landscape in the alternating and classic layouts, square in the small strip beneath a classic lead, and a mix of portrait and square in the gallery layout. A very wide photo loses its left and right edges; a very tall one loses its top and bottom. Anything important at the very edge of the frame may not survive, so leave a little room around it.
Describe what is happening in the photo, not that it is a photo.
