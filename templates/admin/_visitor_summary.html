Counted on this server. No analytics service and no extra cookie: what is stored is the page, the day, and a one-way hash that is thrown away and remade every night, so two visits on different days can never be joined up. Nothing here identifies anybody, and nothing here can be turned back into a person.

{#- The daily hash is what makes the figures honest AND what limits them, and saying only the first half would be a half-truth on a page of numbers somebody will quote. -#}

A “visit” is one person on one day. Because the hash is remade every night, somebody who comes back next week is counted again — deliberately, since linking the two would be exactly the tracking this avoids. So “visits this month” is not the number of different people who came; it is the number of person-days.
